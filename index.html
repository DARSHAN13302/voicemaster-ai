VoiceMaster.AI is a professional‑grade generative voice studio featuring Speech‑to‑Text, Text‑to‑Speech, and live 4‑tier translation across 40+ global languages including all 22 official Indian languages. The Smart Gender Heuristic intelligently detects and switches between male and female voices, with native availability notifications. Includes 10 emotion modes (Angry, Sad, Whisper, Scream, etc.) and 6 age/pitch settings from Child to Robot. Built with modern UI/UX, CI/CD ready, and designed for accessibility.

Engine

STT & TTS engine powered by Web Speech API. Real‑time translation via MyMemory.

Privacy

No voice data stored. No backend database. Translation requests are anonymized.
